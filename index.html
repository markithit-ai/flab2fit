06 / A few helpful answers

Questions, answered.

What happens in the ₹99 consultation?+

It’s an introductory conversation to discuss your goals, everyday routine, food preferences and nutrition concerns, then explore sensible next steps.

Is this only for weight management?+

No. You can discuss broader nutrition and lifestyle concerns, including food habits, PCOS- or thyroid-related nutrition questions, and everyday wellbeing.

Will I receive a full diet plan during the call?+

The consultation is intended to understand your needs and discuss next steps. A personalised plan or ongoing coaching, if appropriate, may be a separate service.

How do I schedule the meeting?+

Complete the ₹99 payment first. You’ll then be able to choose an available time using the appointment calendar embedded on this page.

Will I receive a confirmation message?

This page does not send a separate confirmation message. Once you complete the calendar booking, Google Calendar handles the appointment event and any settings configured in your booking calendar.

Can this replace medical advice?

No. Nutrition consultation does not replace diagnosis, treatment or advice from your doctor or other qualified healthcare professional.
